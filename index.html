About

My research has followed one line: models that work on real, platform-scale multimodal data, and that are fast and controllable enough to be deployed. It runs in three phases — multimodal understanding and recommendation for industrial content platforms, then efficient video generation, and now streaming and interactive video generation and editing, which is where the rest of my PhD goes. All of it has been done on industrial data, where a model is judged on quality, controllability, speed and cost together.

Research interests: streaming and interactive video generation and editing; few-step distillation; efficient video generation; multimodal representation learning.
